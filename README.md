# ChatJump

A Chrome extension that saves you from endless scrolling in AI chat conversations.

## The Problem

You're deep into a long conversation with ChatGPT or Gemini, and you need to reference something you asked 20 messages ago. What follows is frustrating scroll-scroll-scroll, trying to find that one question buried in walls of AI responses.

AI conversations get long fast. AI responses are often paragraphs or pages of text, making it nearly impossible to quickly find your previous questions. You lose your train of thought. You waste time. You forget what you even asked.

## The Solution

ChatJump adds a sidebar that lists all your questions in the conversation. Click any question to instantly jump to it. No more scrolling.

**Features:**
- **Question Navigator** - See all your questions in a clean sidebar
- **One-Click Jump** - Click to scroll directly to any question
- **Auto-Updates** - Sidebar updates as you continue the conversation
- **Resizable Sidebar** - Drag to adjust the sidebar width
- **Text Highlighting** - Hold Alt/Option + select text to highlight important parts (highlights persist across sessions)

## Supported Platforms

**Full Support (Question Navigator + Highlighting):**
- ChatGPT (chat.openai.com, chatgpt.com)
- Google Gemini (gemini.google.com)
- DeepSeek (deepseek.com, chat.deepseek.com)

**Highlighting Only:**
- Claude (claude.ai)
- Perplexity (perplexity.ai)
- Microsoft Copilot (copilot.microsoft.com)
- Poe (poe.com)
- You.com
- Pi (pi.ai)
- Mistral (chat.mistral.ai)
- Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com)
- HuggingFace Chat (huggingface.co/chat)

## Installation

1. Clone or download this repository
2. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions/`
3. Enable "Developer mode" (top right)
4. Click "Load unpacked" and select the extension folder

## Usage

1. Visit any supported AI chat platform
2. Click the floating 📋 button (top right) to open the question navigator
3. Click any question to jump to it
4. To highlight text: hold Alt/Option and select text with your mouse
